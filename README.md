# **E-Commerce Application**

This is a full-featured **E-Commerce application** built with modern technologies, providing a seamless shopping experience with robust authentication, product management, and order processing.

---

## **Project Structure**

---

## **Tech Stack**

- **Frontend:** React.js, TypeScript, Material-UI (MUI), Redux Toolkit
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js, GraphQL
- **Database:** PostgreSQL with TypeORM
- **State Management:** Redux Toolkit
- **API Communication:** GraphQL
- **Routing:** React Router

---

## **Key Features**

### **User Authentication**

- **Login and Registration:** Secure user authentication.
- **Protected Routes:** Redirect users to the homepage after login.

### **Homepage**

- **Product Slider:** Showcase featured products.
- **Product Listings:** Display products across multiple categories.

### **Category & Subcategory Management**

- **Product Categorization:** Categories like **Groceries**, **Fashion**, and **Electronics**.
- **Subcategories & Companies:** Group products by subcategory and brand.
- **Gender-Based Sections:** Dedicated sections for **Men**, **Women**, and **Kids**.

### **Product Details & Cart**

- **Product Details Page:** View detailed product descriptions, images, and pricing.
- **Add to Cart & Direct Buy:** Add products to the cart or buy directly.
- **Quantity Management:** Increase or decrease product quantity directly in the cart.

### **Checkout Process**

- **Address Management:** Add, edit, and save multiple addresses.
- **Order Summary:** Review items, quantities, and pricing.
- **Payment Option:** Supports **Cash on Delivery (COD)**.

### **Order Tracking**

- **Current Orders:** View ongoing orders and statuses.
- **Order History:** Access previous order records.

### **Admin Panel**

- **Product Management:** Add new products through a form.
- **Database Integration:** Products are saved in **PostgreSQL** via **GraphQL** and **TypeORM**.

### **Dynamic Navigation**

- **Category & Subcategory Pages:** Click categories to filter product listings.
- **Seamless Routing:** Smooth page transitions with **React Router**.
